All research findings and analyses must be supported and verified by source materials. Each researcher must submit a structured report in a professional academic tone using the following eight key areas:

1.  **Explanation of Material Understanding:** A clear explanation of your understanding of the foundational ULS white paper and the critical peer review material.

6.  **Description of Final Product (Data & Method):** A detailed description of the final deliverable, including the generated research data (pilot or simulation) and the improved ULS method or sub-mechanism (functional specification) supported by that data.
7.  **Suggestions for Future Research:** Concrete suggestions for future research that expand upon your findings and address newly identified gaps in the improved methodology.
